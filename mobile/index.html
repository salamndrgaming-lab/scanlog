Audio source

On broadcastify.com go to Tennessee → Shelby County, open “Memphis Police and Shelby County Sheriff” or “Memphis Fire Department”. The number at the end of the page address is the feed ID.

On a computer in Chrome or Edge: play the feed in another tab (e.g. Broadcastify's player), tap Start listening, pick that tab and keep “Share tab audio” on.

Put the phone next to a scanner radio, or next to another device playing a feed. Works with any audio, no setup.

Transcription

Free and private: audio never leaves the phone. Downloads once over Wi-Fi. Newer phones keep up with one busy channel.

Groq (whisper-large-v3-turbo) is fast and cheap; OpenAI works too (endpoint https://api.openai.com/v1, model whisper-1). Most accurate, uses data.

Raise it if static creates junk clips; lower it if quiet voices are missed.
